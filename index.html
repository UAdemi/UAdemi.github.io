Fragmented records, then the relationships between them, then one path through to an outcome. That sequence is the job, whether the setting is research, data, or a business.

One way of working, across different settings

I am completing a Ph.D. in Political Science at Northwestern University and hold an M.S. in Applied Statistics. My work has taken me from developing new methods for causal inference to building large public datasets, growing a consumer business, and communicating complex ideas to broad audiences.

The settings differ, but the underlying challenge is usually the same: the problem is important, the information is incomplete, and there is no ready-made playbook.

Current focus

How should we evaluate a decision when it changes not only outcomes, but also the system through which those outcomes unfold?

My current research develops causal methods for settings in which policies reshape evolving networks. The broader aim is to make credible evaluation possible when interventions, relationships, and behavior change together over time.
