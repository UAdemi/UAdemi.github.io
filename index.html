Current focus

How should we evaluate a decision when it changes not only outcomes, but also the system through which those outcomes unfold?

My current research develops causal methods for settings in which policies reshape evolving networks. The broader aim is to make credible evaluation possible when interventions, relationships, and behavior change together over time.

Read about my research

Emerging focus

Human judgment in AI-mediated systems

I am increasingly interested in how people delegate judgment to AI: when AI complements expertise, when it encourages overreliance, and how repeated human–AI interaction reshapes trust, behavior, and institutions.
